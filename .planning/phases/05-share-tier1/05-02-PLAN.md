<objective>
Add a "Partajează" entry in the Copii screen that opens a share sheet. The
sheet lets the parent (a) pick which individual child(ren) to share, and (b)
toggle a checkbox "Trimite toată aplicația" which overrides per-child
selection and shares everything (all children + any custom medications).
On confirm, show the generated URL with copy + native share buttons.

Purpose: This is the on-ramp to the parent-to-parent share use case.
Output: A `ShareSheet` component + integration in `ChildrenScreen`.
</objective>
