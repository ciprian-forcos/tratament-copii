# Purpose

This node records public references as background context for
[Treatment plan rules](../medical/treatment-plan-rules.md). The app is a small
home-helper/prototype and does not supersede doctor recommendations, product
leaflets, pharmacist advice, or emergency guidance.

# Public Guidance Observed

The sources reviewed generally describe:

* paracetamol for children with at least 4 hours between doses and maximum 4 doses per 24 hours,
* ibuprofen for children commonly every 6 to 8 hours or with per-day maximums,
* caution when alternating paracetamol and ibuprofen, usually giving one medicine at a time and tracking what was given.

These public sources are not intended to prove or disprove the project's chosen
simplification. They help future contributors understand the surrounding domain
without pretending the repo is a clinical authority.
